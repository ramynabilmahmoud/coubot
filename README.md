# Coubot 🤖

**Coubot** is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to interface with an autonomous food delivery robot. The app provides a seamless dining and shopping experience by allowing users to browse menus, order food or drinks, and track their delivery robot's journey right to their doorstep.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

*   **Smart Shopping Cart:** Easily browse available food, drinks, and other essentials, and add them to your cart with a single tap.
*   **Secure Purchasing:** Fast and secure checkout system to buy meals, snacks, beverages, and daily necessities.
*   **Real-Time Robot Tracking:** Track the physical delivery robot's status and live location as it navigates to your delivery point.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

*   **Framework:** Flutter (Dart)
*   **Platform:** Android & iOS
